What “PPSR listed” means

Some of our cars are PPSR listed. That means the car was once assessed as a repairable write-off, has since been professionally repaired, and has passed the required government inspection before it could be registered again. We say so on every car it applies to, in that car’s description. It’s why the price is lower than a car with no history.

You’re welcome to bring an RAA or any independent inspector before you buy, and we’ll show you the PPSR report on request.

Before you enquire

Pricing. Our prices are set after comparing the market, so they’re generally firm. Rego, stamp duty, government charges and transfer fees are extra unless the listing says otherwise. Registration and warranty are done on site at sale, so you can drive away the same day.

Condition. Every car is pre-owned and workshop-maintained to a high standard. Some cosmetic wear consistent with age may be present. Pre-purchase inspections are welcome on site.

Warranty. Cars under 15 years old and under 200,000 km come with a 3-month or 5,000 km statutory warranty, whichever comes first. Extended 1, 3 or 5 year warranties are available.

Finance and trade-ins. Finance is available for eligible applicants through our broker. Tell us before you inspect. Trade-ins welcome, no swaps.

Hours. 9 to 5 Monday to Friday, weekends by appointment.

Please research the make and model before enquiring, and be respectful to our staff.
