The formula

Anatomy of a 30-second reel

  • 0–2 sHook — say the symptom to camera. First words name the problem the viewer has: “ABS light flashing while you drive?” Nothing else opens the video.
  • 3–10 sWhat it means. One or two sentences — what's probably wrong and why leaving it costs more later. Plain words, no jargon.
  • 10–25 sShow the fix. Real footage: the car on the hoist, the worn part next to the new one, the scanner reading. Your workshop is the proof.
  • 25–30 sThe same CTA, every time. Word for word — see the black box below.
One video = ONE symptom. Never list five problems in one reel — the person with the ABS light needs to feel like this video was made for them.
Ready to shoot

Ten scripts — film straight off this list

“ABS light flashing while you drive?”

Say: that light means the system that stops you skidding is off duty. Usually a sensor — cheap to check, dangerous to ignore in the wet.

Show: the scanner pulling the fault code, the wheel sensor.

Sells: diagnostics — a real customer already booked from this exact problem
“Brakes squealing when you slow down?”

Say: that squeal is the wear indicator — the pad telling you it's nearly gone. Catch it now and it's pads; leave it and it's discs too.

Show: worn pad next to a new one — the visual sells itself.

Sells: brake inspection / Standard Service
“Car pulling to one side on the highway?”

Say: could be alignment, could be a tyre, could be a brake dragging. Ten minutes on the hoist tells us which.

Show: steering wheel held straight while the car drifts; alignment rig.

Sells: steering & alignment
“Engine stalls right after starting?”

Say: you already made this thumbnail — this is the video version. Cold-start stall is usually air, fuel or an earthing issue. Don't keep cranking it to death.

Show: the stall happening, then the scanner.

Sells: diagnostics & mechanical repairs
“Temperature needle creeping up in traffic?”

Say: overheating kills engines — one warped head costs more than ten cooling repairs. It's usually the fan, thermostat or a slow leak.

Show: coolant pressure test, the stained radiator.

Sells: radiator & cooling
“Aircon blowing warm this summer?”

Say: nine times out of ten it's low gas from a slow leak. A regas with a dye test finds the leak instead of masking it.

Show: the gauges hooked up, vent thermometer before/after.

Sells: air conditioning — post in spring, pin all summer
“Clunk over every speed bump?”

Say: that knock is usually a sway-bar link or bush — small parts that make the whole car feel old. Cheap fix, instant difference.

Show: shaking the worn link by hand on the hoist.

Sells: suspension
“Car slow to crank in the morning?”

Say: batteries don't die suddenly — they warn you for weeks. A two-minute test tells you if it's the battery, the alternator, or a drain.

Show: the battery tester verdict printing.

Sells: battery test / Standard Service
“Oil spots on the driveway?”

Say: a leak is the engine telling you where it hurts. Finding it early is a gasket; finding it late is an engine.

Show: UV dye tracing the leak line.

Sells: servicing & repairs
“Diesel blowing black smoke — or the DPF light's on?”

Say: short trips clog diesels. A forced regen or clean now beats a $4,000 filter later.

Show: the sooty tailpipe, the regen running on the scanner.

Sells: diesel & DPF
The close

Same CTA, word for word, every reel

“If that's your car — link in bio, tap ‘Issues with my car’, tell us what's wrong, and we'll sort it.”

Say it to camera AND put it as text on screen for the last 3 seconds. Repetition is the point — followers should be able to finish the sentence for you. That's when it's working.
