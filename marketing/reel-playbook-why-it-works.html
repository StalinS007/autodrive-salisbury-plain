And the two ends of the funnel agree: Instagram counted 180 bio-link taps; the website's own analytics counted ~176 arrivals from that link. One of them described an ABS fault, booked on WhatsApp, and became a paying customer — the full path, proven once already. The playbook exists to make that happen on purpose, every week.

The door

What happens when they click

The website was rebuilt for exactly these videos. When someone taps the bio link:

Reel→ Link in bio→ “Issues with my car”→ Picks a day→ Make · model · year · km→ Describes the problem→ Lands in your WhatsApp

So the message you receive already reads like this: “Hi Jitty, my car is having some issues and I would like to get it checked. My car is free on Monday 17 August. Car: Mitsubishi Pajero, Year: 2009, Odometer: 317,000 km. Issue: ABS light flashing while driving.” — a quote-ready lead, not a “hey, how much?”
